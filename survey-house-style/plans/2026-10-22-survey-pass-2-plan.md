## Standing answers

From the design: past 270 path minutes, stop and report. A file whose copy differs by any byte keeps its workstation original, and the PR lists it. A contrast failure is tuned within its hue, with old and new values in `## Decisions`. A repo with no prose is left out and listed with its reason.

Plus:

- **Left out, and listed in each PR body.** drafter and approval-queue have no Vale config, and their prose reaches Survey through the workstation links. servers lints with Google alone, so adding Survey would bring new findings, not swap a copy.
- **Vale runs that differ.** A repo whose "after" run differs from its "before" run beyond the rename keeps its `glw907` copy, and its PR is dropped and listed.
- **A reader that breaks on a link.** A file whose link fails a reader check (the register test, a gate leg) stays a regular file, and the PR lists it.
- **A failed `## Before merge` check.** If the tag's files differ from the PR head (Geoff's review changed them), the conductor rebuilds the six dependent PRs from the tag and carries them in a second packet.
- **The output style after the merge.** If `claude -p "/output-style"` doesn't list `writing-voice (current)`, the conductor reverts the output-style link commit on a branch and merges that itself (`output-styles` is `ordinary` in the dotfiles map). It reports in STATUS, and the docs links stay.
- **A stow conflict after the merge.** The conductor leaves the skill unstowed (the links need no stow), files the cause through `log-issue`, and reports.
- **A failing docs gate on the pointer paragraph.** Only the new paragraph is reworded, until it passes.
- **cairn-pass's close checklist.** This is not a cairn pass. It takes cairn-pass's fast lane and CI watch only, and the friction-log triage stays with cairn's next pass (`## Decisions`).
