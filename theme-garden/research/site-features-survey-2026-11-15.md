# Site features by kind, surveyed 2026-11-15

Four read-only surveys of public sites, run to decide what each kind of cairn site starts with.
The bar is "the large majority of sites of that kind" (Geoff, 2026-11-15), about 80 percent, read
with judgment near the line. All four fetched served HTML with no browser, so anything a script
draws is undercounted: search icons, cookie banners, bot checks, dark-mode switches, newsletter
boxes, share buttons. Samples are small and not random.
