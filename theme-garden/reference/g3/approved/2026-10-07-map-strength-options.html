How strong should the map character be?

All three use direction B. They differ only in how much of the topographic map shows through. In each one, the phonebook stays quieter than 907.life, because it's a tool you scan quickly.

Proposed full palette for B (light)

One change from the first screen. Contour brown becomes the secondary, and vegetation green becomes the success color, so green never means two things. The status colors come from USGS map symbols: glacier teal for info, survey ochre for warnings, and benchmark red for errors.
