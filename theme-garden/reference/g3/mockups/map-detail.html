Map detail: Survey sheet or Trace

Left is the default, the Survey sheet you approved as option 2. Right is Trace, your option 1: the contour lines stay in the header, and the rest of the map detail goes. Trace drops the benchmark triangles (beside the site name, on related posts, the banner, and the 404 page), turns the dashed contour rules into plain hairlines, and sets the year headings in ink. Everything else is the same theme.
