Survey's home page, two ways

A theme can restyle cairn's home page but can't rearrange it. The page always opens on a title and description, then the newest post set apart with its summary and a "Read the post" link, then the archive grouped by year.

A keeps that layout and dresses it in Survey. B is the home you approved on the map-strength screen (option 2): the title, then plain date-and-title rows. B needs either a change in cairn so a theme can lay out the home page, or CSS that fights cairn's markup. Both are light mode at desktop width; dark mode, phone widths, and the other pages come next.
