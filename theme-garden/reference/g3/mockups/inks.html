Which ink colors should Survey offer?

The ink sets the color of links, buttons, and the focus ring. Every candidate passes WCAG contrast with room to spare. Each column shows one ink in light and dark: a paragraph with a link, a button, a focused Cancel button (the outline is the focus ring), and a note alert, which keeps its own teal in every column.

Vegetation green is left out because Survey made green the success color so it never means two things. Glacier teal has the same problem, more mildly: it's close to the note alert's teal, as the third column shows.
