## Standing answers

- **A blocking engine defect** goes to `conductor-cairn-cms` right away, and the builder waits. When a fixed candidate ships, the builder resumes on it, and the PR records the version.
- **A builder struggling.** The observer lets it struggle, since struggle is where the gaps show. It steps in only when the builder is truly stuck: the same failure three times running, 30 minutes with no new commit or file, or a denied destructive pattern. Each assist is one hint, and it's logged as a backlog item naming the gap that made it necessary (Geoff, 2026-11-07).
- **A question a newcomer would answer for themselves** is answered from the design, the spec, and the prototype. Only a product, taste, or scope question they don't settle goes to Geoff.
- **A mail check fails, or a mail record changes.** The run stops before anything further runs, and the servers path restores the record (spec, "Mail"). It resumes when the mail check passes.
- **Setup offering a second Workers Builds registration**: Geoff declines it, and the observer tells the conductor.
- **A gap in the theme contract** becomes a backlog item, and the theme carries the smallest workaround, marked with the item's id.
- **A Survey gap** goes into Survey's `BACKLOG.md` as a direct commit.
- **A harness gap**, caused by the clean room rather than the published tools, goes in the fold record, not to the conductor.
- **The usage limit.** The observer waits for the reset and resumes each builder by its session id.
- **A task past twice its estimate** with a traced cause goes on, within the ceiling, and the PR records it. With no traced cause, it gets one hint as above. A cause that returns after its fix gets one structural fix, and if it returns again, the item is escalated in the packet. T9's home page may escalate. T5 through T8 may not.
- **907.life dark after the teardown**, for any length: the run waits for the conductor's fix with no time limit (Geoff, 2026-11-07: "the 907.life site can stay dark as long as it needs to").
- **Spend and path ceiling:** 1,200 path minutes. Past it, or when the bar can't be reached, T11's declarations and P6's token deletions still run. Then the observer writes STATUS, closes, and reports the bar unmet.
