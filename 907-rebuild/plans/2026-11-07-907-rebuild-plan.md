## Standing answers

- **A blocking engine defect** goes to `conductor-cairn-cms` right away, and the builder waits. When a fixed candidate ships, the builder resumes on it, and the PR records the version.
- **A builder struggling.** The observer lets it struggle, since struggle is where the gaps show. It steps in only when the builder is truly stuck: the same failure three times running, 30 minutes with no new commit or file, or about to do damage. Each assist is one hint, and it's logged as a backlog item naming the gap that made it necessary (Geoff, 2026-11-07).
- **A question a newcomer would answer for themselves** is answered from the design, the spec, and the prototype. Only a product, taste, or scope question they don't settle goes to Geoff.
- **Setup offering a second Workers Builds registration**: the builder stops, and the observer tells the conductor.
- **A gap in the theme contract** becomes a backlog item, and the theme carries the smallest workaround, marked with the item's id.
- **A Survey gap** goes into Survey's `BACKLOG.md` as a direct commit.
- **A task past twice its estimate** with a traced cause goes on, within the ceiling, and the PR records it. With no traced cause, it gets one hint as above. A cause that returns after its fix gets one structural fix, and if it returns again, the item is escalated in the packet. T9's home page and T10's domain may escalate. T5 through T8 may not.
- **Spend and path ceiling:** 1,200 path minutes. Past it, the observer writes STATUS and stops.
- **Auto mode refusing a delete in T5**: the observer writes the exact commands to `~/Projects/cleanroom/teardown.sh`, and you run it in Ptyxis. T5 waits on the check.
