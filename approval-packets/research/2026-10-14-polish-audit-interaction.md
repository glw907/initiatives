# Interaction, states, motion, accessibility ledger

Lens: interaction, states, motion, accessibility, measured. Read-only audit; nothing in `site/` was edited. Renders, scripts and the proposed CSS live in `audit/interaction/` (`t*.mjs` drivers, `*.css` proposals, `*.png` renders). Pages driven: the board, `repo-organization--04/06/08`, `approval-queue--01`, and all 13 for layout/title/heading checks. Browser: Chromium via Playwright. Research citations are to `research-decision-ui.md` (section numbers and the ranked list "P1..P14").

Verdict key: already-right / recommend / not-applicable. Impact: high / medium / low.

## Summary

Mostly already-right. The focus system, locked Approve, undo hold, live region, shortcut scoping, reduced motion, landmarks and titles all measure clean. Six real defects, in priority order:

1. Unchecked ask radios have a ring of about 1.5:1 (light) and 1.7:1 (dark), failing WCAG 1.4.11. The theme's own guard is defeated by `radio-sm`.
2. The mobile drawer does not take focus on open while motion is enabled.
3. The sticky bar is 186px (22%) on a 390px phone; a compact two-row treatment measures 104px (12%).
4. At 390px the routine table squeezes the Part column to about 120px and clips the override controls (breaks P8, "visible in-row controls").
5. Board rows highlight on hover but only a 17px text link is clickable.
6. Three small AA misses: routine table header 4.05:1, note placeholder 3.12:1 (both light theme), and CLS from font swap (0.079 on phone; zero with two preloads).

## Top recommendations in priority order

1. **Unchecked ask radio ring** (row 17, high). One rule in `input.css`: `.ask-option .radio:not(:checked){border-color:var(--site-field-border)}`.
2. **Drawer focus on open and close** (row 18, medium). Retry `focus()` until the drawer is visible; return focus on overlay close.
3. **Compact two-row decision bar below 640px** (row 22, medium). CSS in `audit/interaction/bar.css`: 186px to 104px.
4. **Phone routine table and board** (rows 20 and 21, medium and low-medium). CSS in `audit/interaction/table.css` and `board.css`.
5. **Whole board row clickable** (row 23, medium). Stretched link.
6. **AA text patches** (rows 15 and 16, low). Routine header and textarea placeholder colours.
7. **Font preload** (row 24, low). Two `<link rel=preload>` tags; CLS to zero.
8. **Low polish**: undo announcement length (row 10), dead Background hover (row 5), duplicate fieldset description (row 26).
