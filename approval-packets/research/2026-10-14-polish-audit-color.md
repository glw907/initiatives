# Color, surface, depth, and whole-surface coherence ledger

Lens: color, surface, depth, coherence. Read-only. Renders and measurement scripts are in `audit/color/` (`audit-color-measure.mjs` gives every ratio below, computed from composited computed styles in both themes at 1440). Abbreviations: L = light (`survey`), D = dark (`survey-dark`). R-DUI = `research-decision-ui.md`.

The bar for a change is high, and most knobs pass. Four changes are recommended, none of them in the theme files.

## Whole-surface coherence

**One designed system or accretion?** Mostly one system. The page uses two radii assigned by role, one border token plus a field-edge token for controls, and status inks drawn from a contrast-checked token set. Primary is kept for you and actions, the board carries a single hue, and depth comes from tone, not shadow, apart from one raised bar. The traces of accretion are all in dark mode: the bar sinks, the board nav fill vanishes, and the drawer regions sink. Those read as light-first decisions that never got a second look in dark, not as a second design language. The other trace is the summary rail, which carries the alert device onto a box that isn't an alert.

**AI-default clusters?** The cream-serif-terracotta cluster doesn't fire. The ground is a neutral-warm #f7f7f3, the accent is steel blue, red appears only for irreversible, and the serif/sans/mono trio is the owner's Survey theme. The broadsheet hairline cluster doesn't fire either: rules are sparse and functional (dashed layer dividers, one footer rule, group rules). The bar's gradient is a functional scroll fade, not a hero. The page has no emoji markers, only line icons that always carry a word. The uniform-rounded-cards-with-accent-rails cluster is the only near miss. The asks and Not checked use the theme's alert idiom, where a status rule on a field means status, and that's coherent. The summary box's primary rail is what tips the brief column toward "every box gets a rail" (row 14).

## Top recommendations, in priority order

1. **Remove the primary rail from the summary box** and widen its start padding to keep alignment (`build.mjs:514`: drop `border-s-4 border-primary`, `ps-4` to `ps-5`, `lg:ps-6` to `lg:ps-7`). Medium. Backed by R-DUI 4.5 (one feature per meaning; the primary left rule already means "changed row") and 1.5 (the fill alone is the common region). After the change, a left rule means status everywhere.
2. **Lift the sticky bar in dark mode**: base-200 fill and a heavier shadow under `[data-theme='survey-dark'] .bar` and the `prefers-color-scheme` twin. Medium. In D the bar now reads darker than the ask tints it passes over.
3. **Make the board's nav active fill visible in dark**: `#queue-nav > .btn-active { --btn-bg: var(--color-base-300); --btn-border: var(--color-base-300); }`. Low.
4. **Set a theme-tinted selection color**: `::selection { background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--color-primary) 24%, transparent); }`. Low.
