# Typography and rhythm ledger

Lens: typography and rhythm. Read-only audit of the static prototype at http://127.0.0.1:34473/ (board plus repo-organization--03, --04, --08, approval-queue--01). Measurements come from `getComputedStyle` and per-character line rects with every disclosure open (`audit/typography/measure.mjs`, output `measure-1440.json` and `measure-390.json`). Candidate fixes were tried by injecting CSS at render time, with no source edits (`experiment.mjs`, `exp2.mjs` to `exp4.mjs`). Before/after renders are in `audit/typography/`.

Dose: standard by default. A row marked **deep** cites a finding in `research-decision-ui.md` and changes composition inside the approved bones. Where a rule conflicts with the survey theme, the theme wins.

Overall verdict: the type system is mostly right. The face pairing, heading hierarchy, spacing ladder, eyebrow tracking, tabular figures and identifier sizing all hold. The real defects are measured ones. Karla's `ch` runs the brief's "65ch" lines to 84–90 characters. The board's packet column falls to 15 characters per line on a phone. Monaspace is faux-bolded wherever it is set at 600.

## Top recommendations, in priority order

1. **Board on a phone: give the initiative its own line** (deep; research 4.1, 1.2, 1.3, principle 9). Grid the rows under 640px. Headlines go from 15 to 30 chars/line, sideways scroll goes away, and the page goes from 2306 to 1375px. High at 390.
2. **Brief measure: 65ch → 56ch** (deep, contestable; research 1.3 and principle 2). Karla's `ch` makes 65ch hold 84–90 characters. 56ch gives about 75. The cost is +28 to +53px, which hides the Not checked heading behind the bar at 1440×900 on long briefs but keeps the whole brief visible at 1920×1080. Owner call. Medium.
3. **Secondary prose measure → 56ch** (Not checked body, ask summary, layer bodies; research 1.3). These run 78–90 chars today, and narrowing them costs the fold nothing. Low to medium.
4. **Board rows at 1rem** (research 8.1, 3.3). The rows currently sit below their own 16.8px subtitle. At 1rem the board still fits one screen at 1440×900. Medium.
5. **Stop faux-bold Monaspace**: `html { font-synthesis-weight: none }` (drawer repo labels, bar kbds). Medium to low.
6. **Curly quotes and apostrophes** in `inline()` and the UI strings. Low to medium.
7. **`text-wrap: pretty` on layer, fact, option and detail text**, attach the board's `·` separator to "Irreversible", and keep ISO dates unbroken. Low.
8. Low: bar kbd to Karla, routine table head to match the board head, no tracking on mono.
