Every screen in the order an owner meets them. Each uses only what Lip Gloss and Bubbles can draw: a cell grid, foreground colour, bold, one rounded border, a spinner, and a bar. The colours are the tool's Warm Stone palette on a dark terminal.

What is a stand-in here. These are drawings, so the real terminal will differ in font. The wording is placeholder for an editor to write. Survey's colours and description are invented, since Survey is not in the catalog yet, and Waymark's specimen colours are approximate. The failed step's output lines and the ten-minute figure are invented too.
